    How it works

    1. Add sources. Paste notes, upload a transcript, an interview recording or a screen recording. Media is cut into small pieces in your browser before upload.
    2. Generate. An AI drafts the SOP. Our checks then compare every step with your sources.
    3. Review. Steps are marked Backed by source, Seen on screen only or Needs staff confirmation. Answer the questions and regenerate.
    4. Export. Word or Markdown. If required questions are open, the document is marked DRAFT.

    Privacy

    Nothing is stored on our server: no accounts, no database, no files kept. Your work stays in this browser.

    Audio (speech to text) and the redacted text (drafting the SOP) are sent to Groq's free tier. Screen recordings are read in your browser with Tesseract.js: screenshots are never uploaded, only the recognised text. Emails, phone numbers, card numbers, Aadhaar, PAN and passwords are masked before text is sent to the AI and in every result.

    Please don't upload confidential or personal data.
